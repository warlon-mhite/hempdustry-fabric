## If you are putting Hempdustry in a modpack

**The ordinary case is fine, including a pack that earns money.** A CC licence only governs acts that
need permission under copyright — copying, distributing, adapting. If your pack lists Hempdustry and
the launcher downloads it from Modrinth, you have not copied anything, and neither the art nor the
music asks anything of you. Ad revenue, a monetised launcher, a subscription server: none of that
changes it, because none of it is an act the licence reaches. Playing the mod is not either.

**Copying, redistributing and adapting are already permitted — the licences just attach conditions.**
You only need to come and ask when your use falls outside them, which in practice means one thing:
it is commercial.

| You want to… | Then |
|---|---|
| List the mod; the launcher fetches it from Modrinth | **Nothing owed** — no copy is made, so no licence is engaged |
| Run it on a server, monetised or not | **Nothing owed** |
| **Embed the jar** in a pack archive, or mirror it | **Go ahead**, non-commercially. Keep this file with it and pass the licences on |
| Ship a **modified** jar | **Go ahead**, non-commercially. Publish your source (AGPL), and §13 if it is network-facing. Art or music you *changed* stays CC BY-NC-SA |
| Fork the mod and publish it | **Go ahead**, non-commercially. Same conditions |
| Use the music or art **outside the game** — a fan video, a wiki, a review, a thumbnail | **Go ahead**, non-commercially. Credit the track as *Nefuß — Moonlight* / *Robadob*, or the art to Hempdustry, and link the licence |
| Do any of the above **commercially** — merchandise, a paid product, anything primarily for commercial advantage | **This is the one that needs permission.** For the music, ask Nefuß; for the rest, ask the author |

Two things people commonly get wrong about ShareAlike, in your favour:

- **It only applies to adaptations.** Redistributing the jar unchanged does not make your pack, your
  video or your website CC BY-NC-SA. Only material you actually *adapt* carries the licence onward.
- **It is not the AGPL.** Your own mod sitting alongside this one in a pack is not an adaptation of
  anything.

Non-commercial is judged on **the use, not the user**, and on the primary purpose of that use — a
non-profit can fall foul of it and a company need not. If your case is genuinely unclear, ask; the
answer is usually yes.
